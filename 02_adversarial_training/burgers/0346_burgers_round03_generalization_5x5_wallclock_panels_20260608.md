## Clarification - round03 dataset meaning

Observed from `tools/generate_burgers_loss3_selective_generalization.py` lines 170-230 and 403-457: these 50 round03 generalization datasets were generated by selecting 50 base samples from the original Burgers train/test split, running a raw `loss3` adversarial attack on those samples, saving the attacked inputs/targets as candidate datasets, scoring the candidate pool, and then copying the top 50 into `round_03/burgers` as selected datasets.

Observed from `generalization_datasets_burgers_loss3_selective_search/round_03/selected_candidate_scores.csv`: the 50 selected datasets have only six semantic generation-setting combinations: `test, eps=0.12, steps=10` (10 datasets), `train, eps=0.12, steps=10` (10), `test, eps=0.10, steps=8` (10), `train, eps=0.10, steps=8` (10), `test, eps=0.08, steps=8` (7), and `train, eps=0.08, steps=8` (3). Therefore many subplot semantic titles are intentionally identical if numeric selected/candidate identifiers are omitted; the datasets differ by sampled base examples, random attack/jitter realization, and selection score, not by separate `kernel/transform/scale/shift` names.

Inference: the neutral semantic names such as `burgers_far_sawtooth_add_scale0p3_shift0` cannot be assigned to these round03 curves without changing the plotted dataset root, because those names refer to `generalization_datasets_rmse_1p5_3x_all_ns50/burgers`, not to the attack-generated round03 root.

## Validity Caveat - broad generalization claim

Observed evidence now makes the interpretation narrower than the original wording: these panels visualize the round03 loss3-selective adversarial stress set, not 50 independent neutral semantic Burgers generalization distributions. The 50 selected datasets are attack-generated from original train/test samples and collapse to six source/epsilon/step configurations; many semantic-only labels repeat for that reason.

Inference: the panels remain useful for inspecting loss behavior on the constructed loss3-selected stress distribution, but they should not be presented as strong evidence that loss3 broadly generalizes across 50 unrelated semantic distribution shifts. Use `docs/burgers_round03_attack_generated_generalization_validity_caveat_20260608.md` as the interpretation caveat.
